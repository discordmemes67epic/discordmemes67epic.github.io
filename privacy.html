Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

yt-meme-agent ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

The app requests only the youtube.upload permission, which it uses to upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's, and it is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
